Our Story

Built for India's Artisans & NGOs

A platform born from belief — that handmade deserves a home.

“We believe every artisan's story deserves to be told, and every NGO's cause deserves to be heard.”

What originated as an undergraduate academic field project at Thakur Shyam Narayan Degree College has grown into a living, community-driven digital bridge. We observed first-hand how rural artisans struggled with mediator exploitation and how impactful local NGOs lacked online visibility. HastKala was created to replace middlemen with direct connection, authentic storytelling, and fair empowerment.

✦ 100% Direct Artisan Earnings
Living Heritage

Preserving Generational Artistry for Modern Patrons

Every handloom weave, terracotta vessel, and brass figurine carried on HastKala represents centuries of community knowledge. By eliminating exploitative intermediaries, we ensure over 85% of retail value flows directly to the creators and their community self-help groups.

The Problem We Solve

Before HastKala

  • ❌ Middlemen taking up to 70% of artisan profits, leaving creators underpaid.
  • ❌ Rural craft clusters isolated from nationwide digital marketplace consumers.
  • ❌ Grassroots NGOs struggling with high fundraising costs and opaque distribution.
  • ❌ Buyers unable to verify genuine artisan provenance versus factory imitations.

With HastKala

  • ✅ 100% direct remuneration straight to artisans with zero mediator cuts or hidden deductions.
  • ✅ Modern digital catalog and storytelling profiles showcasing rural mastery to India and beyond.
  • ✅ Zero-commission donation processing with transparent impact cards and direct volunteer signups.
  • ✅ Certified artisan profiles, craft backgrounds, and direct enquiry channels ensuring authentic heritage.

The Project

🎓

Academic Roots

Conceived as an applied community field project at Thakur Shyam Narayan Degree College, studying direct socio-economic impacts on artisan livelihoods.

💻

Open Source Foundation

Crafted purely with accessible open-source web standards: HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, and vanilla JavaScript for universal cross-device accessibility.

🚀

Free Deployment

Deployed on Vercel with modern edge infrastructure at zero hosting cost, proving sustainable scalability for mission-driven non-profits.
