The Story Behind This Piece

“Every swirl of cobalt is painted by hand without stencils or tracing — an art passed from mother to daughter across generations in Kot Jewar.”

Jaipur blue pottery is uniquely distinguished because it is the only pottery tradition in the world crafted entirely without clay. The dough is formulated using crushed quartz stone powder, recycled glass, Fuller’s earth (multani mitti), and natural tree resin gum. Master artisan Riya Patel hand-presses this special dough into open terracotta moulds, air-dries each piece under the desert sun, and brushes intricate floral arabesques using cobalt and copper oxide mineral pigments before a slow open-pit firing.
