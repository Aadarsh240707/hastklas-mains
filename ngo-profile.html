Our Mission

GreenRoots Foundation bridges ecological preservation and rural craftsmanship. We empower traditional artisan communities across Northern India to replace toxic synthetic chemicals with 100% organic forest-based dyes, clean solar kilns, and regenerative agroforestry practices. By restoring circular supply chains, we ensure sustainable livelihoods while defending indigenous ecosystems.

What We Do

  • ✅ Organising zero-waste natural indigo and madder root harvesting cooperatives across 18 Himalayan villages.
  • ✅ Installing clean solar-powered spinning wheels and rainwater filtration units for handloom artisan clusters.
  • ✅ Providing fair-wage certification, organic traceability, and direct e-commerce connectivity on HastKala.
