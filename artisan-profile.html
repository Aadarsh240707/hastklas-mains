About Me

Born into a traditional lineage of Jaipur potter families, I grew up surrounded by the steady hum of clay wheels and the intoxicating fragrance of wet desert soil. Over the past twelve years, I have dedicated myself to keeping alive ancient Rajasthani porous clay throwing traditions while tailoring designs for modern conscious households. Every piece I shape celebrates handmade patience, honoring unglazed natural finishes that allow liquids and plants to breathe naturally without synthetic coatings.

My Craft

My technique relies on indigenous alluvial riverbed clay sifted with fine desert sand and quartz dust to prevent cracking during firing. Each vessel is individually hand-thrown on a manual stone wheel, paddled with wooden anvils for wall strength, and sun-dried under the Rajasthan sun before slow open-fire baking in wood and husk kilns. The resulting natural terracotta micro-porosity cools stored drinking water naturally and infuses food with wholesome earthy minerals that industrial ceramics can never replicate.
